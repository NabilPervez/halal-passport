# Masjids and Mosques in Denton & DFW Area

The HTML you provided was for the Google Maps interface but didn't contain the raw list of saved places. Based on the coordinates and typical search results for Denton, TX, here is a list of the prominent Masjids and Mosques in the area:

## Denton, TX
* **Islamic Society of Denton (Denton Masjid)**
  * **Address:** 1105 Greenlee St, Denton, TX 76201
  * **Details:** Established in 1981, it is noted for being the first masjid built from the ground up in the state of Texas. It serves as the primary spiritual and community hub for Muslims in Denton, including students from the University of North Texas (UNT) and Texas Woman's University (TWU).
  * **Website:** [dentonmasjid.com](https://www.dentonmasjid.com)

## Major Mosques in the DFW Metroplex
The DFW metroplex is home to over 60 mosques. Below are some of the most prominent centers:

* **Islamic Association of North Texas (IANT) – Richardson**
  * **Address:** 840 Abrams Rd, Richardson, TX
  * **Details:** One of the oldest and largest mosques in North Texas (established 1971), serving thousands of worshippers and featuring a community center, medical clinic, and the IANT Quranic Academy.
* **EPIC Masjid (East Plano Islamic Center) – Plano**
  * **Address:** 1350 Star Ct, Plano, TX 75074
  * **Details:** Known as one of the most inclusive and active centers in the region, offering extensive youth programs and adult classes.
* **Valley Ranch Islamic Center – Irving**
  * **Address:** 9940 Valley Ranch Pkwy W, Irving, TX 75063
  * **Details:** A highly active community hub located centrally in the DFW area.
* **Masjid Al-Islam – Dallas**
  * **Address:** 2604 S Harwood St, Dallas, TX
  * **Details:** Historically significant as the oldest Muslim community in the DFW area.

* **[Zabihah](https://www.zabihah.com):** Often used to find local mosques and halal dining options.
* **[Muslim Guide](https://muslimguide.com):** Provides a comprehensive directory of Islamic centers by city.
